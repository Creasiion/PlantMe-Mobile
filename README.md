# PlantMe-Mobile
There are plenty of guides online as to how to take care of a plant, but not all of them work. There’s a learning curve for beginners to begin taking care of their plants, such as what species to buy, how often to water them, the necessary temperature to keep them alive, and many more. Not only that, but a problem that arises for both beginners and experienced plant owners is having a schedule that works for their everyday life as well as the plant’s life. Our app, PlantMe, will solve these problems by taking care of your plant more immersive, with tools included to help you take care of the plants you love with a busy life!
